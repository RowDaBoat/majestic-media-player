What makes the AppImage run on any Linux distribution (tools/appimage.sh)
=========================================================================

Not part of the player: the AppImage's runtime (uruntime), the launcher that
loads the bundled libraries (sharun), the library it preloads to keep the
programs the player starts out of them (anylinux.so, from Anylinux-AppImages)
and cross-libc-dlopen. All under the MIT license, below. The dash shell sharun
uses for its scripts comes with its own license: share/licenses/dash-COPYING
inside the AppImage.
